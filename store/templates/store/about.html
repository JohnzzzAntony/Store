Our Story

Established in 2006, Perfumes has grown into a symbol of luxury, craftsmanship, and artistry — with a clear vision: to create premium perfumes that embody elegance, depth, and timeless appeal.

At Perfumes, fragrance is more than a product. It is an expression of identity, culture, and personal story. We combine carefully selected ingredients with meticulous craftsmanship, positioning Ecom as a unique olfactory experience for clients in the UAE and beyond.

"Fragrance is the invisible part of your personality that announces your arrival before you even speak."

— Perfumes

Our Craft

Every Ecom fragrance is the result of a painstaking creative process. Our master perfumers source the world's finest raw materials — from the oud forests of Southeast Asia to the rose valleys of Bulgaria — and blend them with uncompromising precision.

Each bottle is a work of art, designed to complement the precious liquid it holds. We believe that luxury lies in the details, from the weight of the flacon to the first whisper of scent that rises from the skin.

Our Heritage

Rooted in the rich perfumery traditions of the Arabian Peninsula, Perfumes draws inspiration from centuries of oud burning, incense crafting, and attar distillation. We honour this heritage while embracing contemporary artistry and global influences.

Our Dubai home — the gateway between East and West — is reflected in every fragrance: a harmonious blend of tradition and modernity, warmth and freshness, depth and lightness.
